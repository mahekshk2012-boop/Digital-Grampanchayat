About Digital Grampanchayat

Committed to sustainable rural development, 100% digital literacy, and transparent e-governance for every citizen.

Our Vision (दृष्टिकोन)

To build a smart, self-reliant, clean, and digitally empowered village where every citizen accesses public welfare schemes, administrative certificates, and grievance redressal without geographical or bureaucratic hurdles.

100% Digital Service Delivery Target

Our Mission (ध्येय)

Digitize all Gram Panchayat records, eliminate paper delays, ensure 24/7 online grievance resolution within 72 hours, foster women empowerment through Self Help Groups (SHGs), and implement eco-friendly water harvesting and solar energy projects.
